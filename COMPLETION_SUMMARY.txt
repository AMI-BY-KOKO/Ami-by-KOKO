🎮 GAMEPLAY FLOW (VERIFIED END-TO-END)

1. Child opens /word-builder
2. Selects language (English/Yorùbá/Français)
3. Home screen shows:
   - Compact header with stats
   - Kòkò character greeting
   - Current level progress
   - Prominent "BUILD A WORD" card
   - Word Garden visual
4. Taps "BUILD A WORD"
5. Sees:
   - Word image/clue
   - Shuffled letter tiles
   - Empty word slots
6. Selects letters in order → snap into place
7. Gets progressive hints if needed
8. Checks answer → Kòkò celebrates ✓
9. Earns 1–3 stars (based on attempts)
10. Word added to My Words
11. Word Garden grows (+1 seed)
12. Streak continues (or resets after 1-day gap)
13. Progress persists (Supabase)
14. Next day: streak counter updates
